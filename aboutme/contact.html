Contact questions

Good to know before we talk.

Can I contact you for a free audit?
This website does not promise a free audit. A consultation can be used to clarify the highest-priority question and whether a paid audit or advisory engagement is appropriate.
Do you need access to my website?
Not to perform an initial conversation or high-level review. If an engagement begins, define the specific access required, how it is stored, and when it is removed.
What happens after I contact you?
I’ll review the website, goals, and main challenge first, then reply with a suggested next step. A formal engagement begins only after the scope, access, and deliverables are agreed.
Do you work internationally?
State your actual service model, time zones, languages, invoicing, and payment methods. Do not imply availability that you cannot support.
