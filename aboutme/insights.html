Editorial standard

Every article should earn its place.

Before publishing, answer the question directly, show relevant experience, cite reliable sources, and explain when the advice may not apply.

01

Original insight

Include analysis, data, screenshots, or lessons that cannot be copied from a generic SEO checklist.

02

Clear authorship

Show who wrote the article, reviewed it, and when it was last fact-checked or updated.

03

Reliable sources

Use official documentation and credible research. Avoid turning unverified third-party claims into facts.

04

Useful next step

Give readers a checklist, question, or action they can apply—without forcing a sales pitch.
