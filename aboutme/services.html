Service questions

Before an engagement starts.

Can services be customized?
Yes. The engagement can be scoped around the outcome, current website condition, available team capacity, and the decisions that need to be made first. A consultation clarifies the smallest useful starting point.
What access do you need?
A review can begin with a public URL. Deeper work may require CMS, hosting, analytics, Search Console, Google Business Profile, or relevant sales access, with permissions limited to what is needed for the agreed scope.
Do you work with agencies?
Collaboration can include direct client work or collaboration with an existing team when the responsibilities, communication path, and quality-control process are clear.
How are recommendations prioritized?
A useful order considers business impact, search potential, effort, risk, dependencies, and the capacity of the team responsible for implementation.

Not sure what you need?

Start with the problem, not a package.

Share your goals and current situation. The right engagement should be based on the outcome you need—not a predetermined sales formula.
